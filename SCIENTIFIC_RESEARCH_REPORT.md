## Scientific evidence and limits

**PTSD:** VA/DoD and NICE endorse clinician-delivered trauma-focused CPT, PE and EMDR; ANEIRA does not reproduce these as self-guided trauma processing. NICE limits supported computerized trauma-focused CBT to defined cases and professional support.

**IPV:** Cochrane synthesized 33 trials/5,517 participants of psychological interventions for IPV survivors. These trials do not validate ANEIRA’s individual exercises.

**Breathing:** Fincham et al. 2023 found pooled subjective stress Hedges g −0.35 (95% CI −0.55 to −0.14) in 12 RCTs/785 participants, mostly moderate risk of bias. A later placebo-controlled trial underscores that breathing rate alone is not a guaranteed mechanism; no claims of HPA repair.

**Behavioral activation:** Cochrane reviewed 53 RCTs/5,495 participants; mostly low-to-moderate certainty. Activity planning is adapted as a self-help skill, not represented as equivalent to therapist-delivered treatment.

**Sleep:** AASM strongly recommends multicomponent CBT-I; sleep hygiene alone is insufficient. ANEIRA offers diary and wind-down tools, not unsupervised sleep restriction or a claim to provide full CBT-I.

**Writing:** a 44-RCT network meta-analysis reports heterogeneous outcomes across writing modalities. ANEIRA journals are optional present-focused reflection, not compulsory traumatic disclosure.

**Identity, spiritual autonomy, attachment and consent:** selected exercises are theory- and guidance-informed. No independent efficacy estimates exist for these ANEIRA-specific implementations.

## Evidence scoring algorithm

Weighted five-part rubric: study design 30%; methodological quality 20%; IPV-specific relevance 20%; consistency 15%; guideline support 15%. Each component rated 1–5, then divided by five and multiplied by 100. Scores are **internal prioritization of source relevance, not measured efficacy or quality-assured GRADE ratings**. Source-level rubrics are attached to each exercise. WHO practice guidance and ITQ assessment sources are capped at 62 because they do not test exercise efficacy. Reuse of a source does not prove a specific ANEIRA activity works.

## Journal library design

41 distinct activities spanning judgment, identity, strengths, grief, attachment, coercion, boundaries, sexual choice, confidence, values, reminders, spiritual meaning, independence, accomplishments, future goals, interests, shame, emotional expression, relationships, work, sleep and pain. Each journal has three tailored prompts, two optional follow-ups, one-screen-at-a-time delivery, explicit save/discard and no compulsory trauma narrative.

## Interactive recommendation and safety governance

1. Survivor selects current goals and preferred exercise formats. No mandatory questionnaire or diagnosis.
2. `recommender.py` normalizes goals, matches activity domains, filters arousal suitability, excluded activities and previously uncomfortable exercises.
3. Transparent ranking uses selected-domain match and modest source-priority signal; voluntary helpfulness is a tie modifier, never a medical predictor.
4. User can skip, stop, decline tracking, change goals, request alternatives and delete content.
5. No traumatic-memory reprocessing, exposure protocol, EMDR, clinician CPT worksheets or unsupervised sleep restriction are generated.
6. If user signals immediate danger or acute crisis, show neutral, region-appropriate support options and quick-exit; never contact police or third parties without explicit user direction.
7. No automatic sharing of journal text; threat model must account for abusive partners accessing devices and cloud backups.

## ANEIRA V3 integration contract

Navigation: Understanding Your Abuse → Recovery Activities → Choose What Helps → Exercise Screen → Optional Reflection → Progress; separate Private Journal and Preferences/Privacy controls. Preserve existing approved homepage; no modification in this package.

Screens: (a) domain/goal chooser, (b) exercise explanation and evidence language, (c) one action at a time with optional timers and accessibility controls, (d) optional helpfulness and finish/skip, (e) private progress history and delete/export, (f) journals with private drafts.

API proposals (not deployed): `GET /recovery/exercises`, `POST /recovery/recommendations`, `POST /recovery/activity-log`, `GET /recovery/journals`, `POST /recovery/journal-draft`, `DELETE /recovery/user-data`. Do not transmit journal text to an AI model without granular consent.

Data fields: pseudonymous user id; optional goal codes; arousal preference; exercise id; completion state; optional helpfulness 0–5; optional user-rated distress before/after; timestamp; version of exercise; optional encrypted journal draft. Separate clinical measures table with instrument version, consent and permissions status.

Privacy requirements: local-first optional storage, encrypted-at-rest cloud only if explicitly opted in, authenticated access, delete/export, no third-party analytics of trauma content, emergency exit and neutral notification names, avoid location collection. Accessibility: WCAG-friendly keyboard/screen-reader support, reduced motion, optional audio, no mandatory eye closure or body focus.

## Verification and limits

All 45 exercises and 41 journals are original ANEIRA content derived from cited general methods and the supplied investigations; they have not undergone an IPV-specific clinical trial or clinical expert content validation. The database distinguishes strong evidence for established treatment families from preliminary evidence for the app’s specific micro-exercises. The executable code is a self-contained research-stage recommendation component; the existing ANEIRA V3 app is unchanged.
